A LITTLE MORE ABOUT ME

Hi, I’m Nicole.
I like untangling things.

I’m most interested in the part of design where things are confusing at first. There’s too much information, too many steps, or no clear next move. That’s the kind of problem I like working through.

My work focuses on app interfaces, design systems, and data-heavy experiences. At Yogger, that meant creating structure around movement assessments, scores, and clinical reports.

Outside of design, I’m drawn to running, yoga, and photography. They remind me to slow down, pay attention, and make small adjustments with intention.

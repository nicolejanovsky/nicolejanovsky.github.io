A LITTLE MORE ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Nicole.

I'm a product designer with a background in visual design, and I love getting to work on both sides of a project: figuring out how something should work and making it look really good.

My experience spans app design, websites, branding, and everything in between. Working across different areas of design has given me a strong visual foundation and a better understanding of how all the pieces come together.

I'm naturally curious, pretty detail-oriented, and always thinking about what could be better. I love brainstorming, getting honest feedback, and working with people who bring different ideas to the table. That's the kind of creative environment I'm excited to be part of.

Outside of work, I'm a 200-hour certified yoga teacher and a wedding videographer. Both give me a chance to explore creativity in completely different ways.
